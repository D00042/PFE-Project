=== TUNISIAN COMMERCIAL COMPANIES CODE (Law No. 2000-93) ===
=== RELEVANT ARTICLES FOR FINANCIAL ANALYSIS ===

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 5 - Share Capital as Creditor Guarantee
-------------------------------------------------------------
Contributions may be in cash, in kind, or in services. All
contributions except services constitute the company's share
capital. The share capital is the exclusive guarantee for
company creditors.
Relevance: Balance Sheet — equity and total assets monitoring.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 13 - Mandatory Statutory Auditor
-------------------------------------------------------------
All commercial companies must appoint a statutory auditor.
Companies other than joint-stock companies are exempt if they
do not meet at least two of the following thresholds: total
balance sheet value, total revenue excluding tax, and average
number of employees.
Relevance: Balance Sheet — total assets threshold monitoring.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 13 QUINQUIES - Financial Statement Compliance
-------------------------------------------------------------
Directors and financial officers must sign an annual
declaration confirming that the financial statements have been
prepared in full compliance with applicable accounting
legislation.
Relevance: All dashboards — general compliance context.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 27 - Equity Below Half of Share Capital
-------------------------------------------------------------
A company may be dissolved when its equity falls below half
of its share capital due to recorded losses. The legal
representative must convene a general assembly to decide on
either dissolution or continuation with financial
regularisation.
Relevance: Balance Sheet — CRITICAL flag if equity ratio
drops below 50%.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 128 - Financial Statement Approval Deadline (SARL)
-------------------------------------------------------------
The annual general meeting must be held within six months of
the close of the financial year. Financial statements must be
communicated to shareholders at least thirty days before the
meeting.
Relevance: All dashboards — reporting deadline compliance.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 256 BIS - Mandatory Audit Committee
-------------------------------------------------------------
A permanent audit committee is mandatory for companies whose
consolidated balance sheet exceeds 100 million Tunisian
dinars. The committee monitors internal controls, financial
reliability, and legal compliance.
Relevance: All dashboards — internal control context.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 266 - Auditor Scope of Investigation
-------------------------------------------------------------
The statutory auditor must verify the company's books, cash
position, portfolio, and securities. They certify the
accuracy of annual accounts and periodically assess the
effectiveness of the internal control system.
Relevance: Balance Sheet and Liquidity — cash and controls.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 275 - Annual General Meeting Obligation (SA)
-------------------------------------------------------------
The ordinary general meeting must be held at least once a
year within six months of the close of the financial year to
review management, approve financial statements, and decide
on profit allocation. Any approval without a prior auditor
report presentation is null and void.
Relevance: All dashboards — annual reporting obligation.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 287 - Distributable Profit Calculation (SA)
-------------------------------------------------------------
The distributable profit equals the net accounting result
adjusted for prior year carry-forwards, after deducting:
5% mandatory allocation to legal reserves until that reserve
reaches 10% of share capital, special legislative reserves,
and statutory reserves. Any resolution in violation is null.
Relevance: Profitability — net profit and retained profit KPIs.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 288 - Dividend Distribution Restriction (SA)
-------------------------------------------------------------
No distribution may be made to shareholders when company
equity is or would become lower than the share capital plus
non-distributable reserves following the distribution.
Relevance: Balance Sheet and Profitability — equity ratio.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 289 - Prohibition of Fictitious Distributions (SA)
-------------------------------------------------------------
Any profit distribution made in violation of Article 288 is
considered fictitious and is prohibited.
Relevance: Profitability — net margin and retained profit.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 307-308 - Capital Reduction Due to Losses
-------------------------------------------------------------
Capital reduction is permitted when losses have reached half
of equity and operations have continued without the asset
base being restored. Reduction may be by reducing number of
shares or their nominal value, per auditor report.
Relevance: Balance Sheet — equity ratio KPI.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 388 - Mandatory Action When Equity Below 50% (SA)
-------------------------------------------------------------
When financial statements show equity has fallen below half
of share capital due to losses, the board must within four
months of accounts approval convene an extraordinary general
meeting to decide on dissolution. If no dissolution is
decided within one year of recording the losses, the company
must reduce or increase capital by at least the amount of
losses. Failure allows any interested party to request
judicial dissolution.
Relevance: Balance Sheet — MOST CRITICAL. Flag if equity
ratio is below 50% with the 4-month deadline obligation.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 471 - Consolidated Financial Statements Obligation
-------------------------------------------------------------
A parent company must prepare consolidated financial
statements in addition to its own annual accounts, audited
by registered statutory auditors.
Relevance: All dashboards — TUI TGBST group reporting.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 472 - Consolidated Statements Publication Deadline
-------------------------------------------------------------
Consolidated financial statements must be made available to
shareholders at least one month before the general assembly
and published within one month of their approval.
Relevance: All dashboards — group reporting deadlines.

-------------------------------------------------------------
ARTICLE 474 - Intra-Group Financial Operations
-------------------------------------------------------------
Financial operations between group companies are permitted
when one holds more than half of another's capital, provided
the operation is commercially justified, not tax-motivated,
has effective counterpart value, and does not serve personal
interests of directors.
Relevance: Liquidity and Balance Sheet — intra-group flows.

FLAG 5 - CRITICAL: If equity would fall below share capital
